How to mark an image
  1. Choose Person or Chair and drag a box around each visible object. Include all its visible parts; stop at the image edge. Do not guess hidden limbs or chair parts.
  2. For partly hidden objects, tick Partly hidden. A seated person and the visible chair are two separate objects. Use Ignore region when an area cannot be judged confidently.
  3. You may use Add YOLO suggestions as a starting point. Correct wrong labels and sizes, delete duplicate/wrong boxes, and add missed objects.
  4. When every visible person and chair is checked, click Confirm reviewed & next. An image with no target objects can be confirmed with zero boxes.
  5. Click Export labels regularly. This downloads your work; the original recording files stay untouched.

Suggestions can bias judgement. Inspect the raw image before using them. Ignore regions are excluded from scoring; use them only for actual uncertainty.

Move mode: click a box to select it, drag inside to move it, or drag its lower-right square to resize. Coordinates below also allow exact editing.
